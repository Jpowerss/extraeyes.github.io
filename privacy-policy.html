Privacy Policy

Last updated November 19, 2026

Overview

Extra Eyes lets you check in from a nearby room with two paired devices or play sounds and stories on one device. You can use the app without an account.

Data We Collect

Extra Eyes uses no analytics, tracking or advertising. The app keeps the stories, voices and preferences you choose to save on your device. Apple handles App Store purchases and optional voice pack downloads.

Camera & Microphone

During monitoring, live video and audio travel directly between the paired Camera and Viewer, encrypted over your local network. Extra Eyes does not record this stream. You can also choose to use the microphone to record a reading or a sample for a family voice.

On-Device AI

Optional cry, person and pet detection runs on the Camera. Extra Eyes also creates family voice profiles and generates narration on the device where you set them up.

Local Network

Extra Eyes asks for Local Network permission to find and connect your Camera and Viewer. Monitoring works over that local connection. Apple provides the optional voice pack as a separate download.

Data Storage

Device backups can include your settings, favorites, bedtime playlist and the text of stories you write. Extra Eyes excludes imported sleep sounds, voice samples, voice profiles and saved readings from backup. Trial status is stored in the Keychain and can sync through iCloud Keychain.

Family Voices and Saved Readings

To set up a family voice, you can record a sample or select audio from Files or a video from Photos. Extra Eyes deletes its working copy of the sample once the voice is ready. Readings you record stay in the app.

With the speaker’s permission, you can export an eligible reading generated from your own story to Files. You choose where to save it, including iCloud Drive. Deleting the voice or reading in Extra Eyes leaves the exported copy in Files.

In-App Purchases

Purchase transactions are handled entirely by Apple's StoreKit framework. Extra Eyes does not process, store, or have access to any payment information.

Children's Privacy

Extra Eyes does not collect any personal information from anyone, including children. The app is designed to be used by parents and caregivers to monitor children, but does not collect data about the children being monitored.

Important Safety Notice

Extra Eyes is not a medical device. It is not intended to detect, diagnose, or treat any medical condition. This app is not a substitute for direct adult supervision. Never leave children unattended.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected in the app and on this page.
