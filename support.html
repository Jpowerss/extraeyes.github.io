Frequently Asked Questions

What can I play at bedtime?

On one iPhone or iPad, open Sleep Stories for classics and nursery rhymes or Sleep Sounds for rain, ocean waves and white noise. You can play 12 English titles read by Emma and all 38 built-in sounds for free. With full access, you can play all 85 titles. Emma’s readings come with the app, ready to play without a separate download.

What devices do I need?

Stories and sounds work on one device. Use two nearby devices for live monitoring, in any combination of iPhone and iPad. Each iPhone needs iOS 26 or later and each iPad needs iPadOS 26 or later. To check your software version, open Settings → General → About.

How do I connect two devices?

Connect both devices to the same local Wi-Fi network. Choose Camera on one and scan its QR code with the Viewer on the other. You can also enter the pairing code manually. Keep Extra Eyes open on the Camera during the session.

Where can I use the monitor?

Use it for short check-ins from a nearby room while an adult is present and supervising. At home or away, both devices need to be on the same local Wi-Fi network. Your own Personal Hotspot also works if your plan supports it. Some hotel and guest networks block connections between devices. Extra Eyes cannot connect to the Camera over the internet.

Keep devices and charging cords out of your child’s reach. The monitor requires adult supervision and is unsuitable for unattended, overnight or medical monitoring.

Is my video stored or sent to any servers?

Live video and audio travel directly between the paired devices, encrypted on your local network. Extra Eyes never records the live stream or sends it to a video server. In Sleep Stories, you choose when to record a reading and save it on that device.

Can I record a reading or use a family voice?

During the trial or with Lifetime Access, choose “Record a reading” to save someone reading aloud in any language. This uses the microphone and works without a Voice Pack.

To have My Voice narrate text in a family member’s voice, download the optional Voice Pack. In Extra Eyes 2.0, My Voice reads English only. It requires full access and at least 5 GB of built-in memory (RAM). The app checks whether your device supports it.

How does the free trial work?

Extra Eyes is free to download. If you opt into the 7-day trial when setting up the Camera or Viewer, it starts when you begin your first paired monitoring session. During the trial, you can monitor live, play all 85 titles and use the reading recorder.

To keep full access, buy Lifetime Access once. It costs $9.99 in the US. The App Store shows your local price. You can keep playing the 12 free titles and 38 built-in sounds after the trial ends.

How do I restore my purchase?

Sign in to the App Store with the Apple Account you used to buy Lifetime Access. Open Settings in Extra Eyes and tap “Restore Purchases.”

Can I add my own sleep sounds?

During the trial or with Lifetime Access, you can import audio from Files and play it as a sleep sound.

What AI detection features are available?

The Camera can detect crying, motion, people and animals during a session. Detection runs on that device. You can choose which alerts to receive in Settings.

The devices won't connect. What should I do?

Check that both devices are on the same Wi-Fi network and that Extra Eyes has Local Network permission on each device. On a guest network that keeps devices apart, try your own Personal Hotspot if your plan supports it. Open Extra Eyes on both devices and pair again.
