Extra Eyes

Play a favorite bedtime story and let gentle rain follow the last page. One iPhone or iPad is enough. For a live check-in from the next room, pair two devices as a private baby monitor.

Download free on the App Store
📖

Bedtime stories with Emma

Start with “Twinkle, Twinkle, Little Star.” Emma reads 85 English classics and nursery rhymes, including 12 free titles. Her readings come with the app, ready to play. Save your child’s favorites in a bedtime playlist.

📱

Check in from the next room

With full access, use one device as the Camera and the other as the Viewer on the same local Wi-Fi or your own Personal Hotspot. See and hear the room, or use Talk to speak back. Use the monitor for short check-ins while you’re nearby and supervising.

🎵

Sleep sounds

Choose rain, ocean waves or white noise and set a sleep timer. All 38 built-in sounds play for free on one device.

🎙

A familiar voice at bedtime

With full access, record someone reading aloud and save it on that device for another night. On a supported device, the optional Voice Pack can also read a story you write in English in a family member’s voice.

🔒

Private live monitoring

Live video and audio travel directly between your paired devices, encrypted on the local network. Extra Eyes never records a live check-in. If you turn on cry or motion alerts, detection runs on the Camera.
